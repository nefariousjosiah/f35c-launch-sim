# Third-party assets

These assets are used by the project but are **not in this repository**. They are
licensed under the Fab Standard License, which allows using them in a project and
in its renders and packaged builds, but forbids redistributing the files. Buy or
claim each one with your own Epic account, then place it in the folder shown. The
folders are ignored by git.

catapult deck kit, materials, effects) or freely licensed content credited in
[ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).

The project still builds and runs without the Fab assets: the launch simulation and
its tests do not depend on them, and missing art falls back to placeholders.
