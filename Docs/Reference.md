## Visual targets ("does it read as CG?")

| CG tell | What real footage shows |
|---|---|
| Bright orange afterburner at noon | Heat shimmer over the jet blast deflector, faint nozzle glow |
| Metallic, F-22-like sheen | Satin-to-matt FS 36170 grey, linear albedo 0.11 to 0.15, slightly warm |
| Solid gold canopy | Clear or bluish; gold or magenta only at angles |
| Fleet grime on a 2014 test jet | Clean VX-23 jet, grey-on-grey STRIKE TEST and NAVY lettering |
| Steam off the jet blast deflector, or drifting forward | Water-cooled JBD, no steam; slot steam blowing aft with the wind |
| A two-second "rocket" stroke | About 2.5 s over 94 m |
| Pilot gripping the stick | Right hand on the towel rack |
| Kneeling nose gear | Strut squat under tension, then oscillation |
| Crew in the wrong colours, standing upright | Yellow shooter crouched, green hookup clear, white checkers, all braced |
| Smooth orbiting camera | Handheld pans with lag, sky near clipping, island telephoto, locked-off bow camera |
| Glossy black deck | Flat dark-grey non-skid with patchy wear, red-and-white foul line |
| Sine-wave sea and a crisp horizon | Sheltered Southern California Bight swell, hazy horizon |
